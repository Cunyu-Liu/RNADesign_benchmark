Source/window overlap & leakage audit (Gate 0, O0-05)

Data: Angenent-Mari 2020 canonical pilot (97,436 rows; 931 target groups).

Key findings

  • Sliding-window identity: adjacent 30-nt trigger windows share a median 0.50 sequence identity (mean 0.42), with 16% of adjacent pairs at ≥0.8 identity.
  • Tiling stride (inferred): {'virus': [1], 'human_TF': [1, 2, 3]}.
  • Random-row-split leakage: 49% of test tiles have a same-source adjacent neighbour in train → random row split leaks near-duplicate windows.

Conclusion

The 91k dataset is tiled with heavy local window overlap. A row-random split places near-identical neighbours on both sides; only a source-disjoint split (S1) prevents source/window leakage.
